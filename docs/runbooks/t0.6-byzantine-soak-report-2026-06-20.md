## Quorum-with-1-jailed observation (separate, expected BFT property)

After M3 was jailed, M1 + M2 continued for **~200 more blocks** then **stalled at h=205** (`Precommit timeout — advancing round` cycling). This is **expected BFT behavior**, NOT a T0.6 bug:

- 3-validator cluster, equal stake (250000 each), total = 750000
- BFT strict quorum threshold = > 2/3 of total = > 500000 → need ≥ 500001
- After M3 jail: M1 + M2 active stake = 500000 = **exactly 2/3, NOT strictly greater**
- Cluster cannot reach strict supermajority with the 2 remaining validators

This is a known property of small BFT clusters: a single slash in a 3-validator set permanently halts forward progress without a governance recovery step (unjail / re-stake / set-size growth). Production mainnet runs ≥ 4 validators where this property doesn't bite.

The T0.6 lane is about **slashing correctness**, not post-slash chain continuity (which is governance territory). Slashing worked correctly; the post-slash halt is the doctrine-expected BFT behavior.
