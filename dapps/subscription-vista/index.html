Subscription Vista

Recurring payment whose lapse detector is the chain itself. pay() is the keep-alive — each call refreshes the contract's energy via the runtime hook; missing payments lets the contract evaporate; on_evaporate flips lapsed = true. No off-chain reaper needed. Mirrors contracts/evaporscript/subscription.es byte-for-byte.

energy below floor. Subscriber has until evaporation to pay; otherwise the contract lapses.
terminal
cancelled (clean) or lapsed (energy hit zero). Distinct doctrinally — a cancelled sub does not relapse.

Energy halves every 100 epochs without payment. Each pay() credits a +200 energy boost — enough to buy roughly log2(1000/200) ≈ 2 additional half-lives of runway. Miss too many consecutive periods and the contract evaporates faster than the cadence can refresh it.

actions
Cancellation is one-shot and blocks future pay() calls. Doctrinally distinct from lapsed-by-evaporation: a cancelled subscription ended cleanly (on_evaporate sees cancelled == true and does NOT flip lapsed).
