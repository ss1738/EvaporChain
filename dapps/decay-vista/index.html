Doctrine: every contract holds a finite energy. Decay is automatic; refresh is opt-in. Below the floor, the contract STILL EXISTS but its view reads "expired" — until the energy hits zero, when the contract evaporates and no method can be called.

This demo runs the canonical energy_at_epoch formula in your browser. Refresh a card to reset its born_epoch back to now — the same op `/api/v1/contract/refresh would perform on a live node.

Math mirrors contracts/evaporscript/{decay_access_pass,mortal_message,mayfly,sap,witnessfit,mnemochain}.es byte-for-byte.
